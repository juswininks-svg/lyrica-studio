Project Summary & GitHub Pages Deployment

Welcome to ୨ৎ LYRICA STUDIO

LYRICA STUDIO is a self-contained browser digital audio workstation built for songwriters to compose lyrics, craft melodies, synthesize electronic singing vocals, layer harmonies, and mix complete tracks without microphones or external APIs.

Quick 5-Step Production Workflow:
1. Play immediately: Press Space or click ▶ in the top transport bar to hear the pre-loaded demo song "Velvet Echoes".
2. Songwriting Room: Edit verses/choruses and click Map Syllables to Melody & Singer.
3. Melody Piano Roll: Click the grid to compose notes or run the deterministic melody generator.
4. Virtual Singer Lab: Shape vowel formants, vibrato, and presets, then click Render Vocal Track to Studio.
5. Mixing & Export: Balance faders and DSP effects, then export a 16/24-bit WAV master.
Shortcuts: Space Play/Pause · Ctrl+S Save · Ctrl+Z Undo

Studio Preferences & Local Storage

Storage Architecture: All projects, lyrics, note events, and mixer settings are stored locally inside your browser's IndexedDB database (LyricaStudioDB). Clearing browser site data removes local projects unless exported as JSON.
